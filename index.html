About Shubham Mhatre

I completed my graduation in Mechanical Engineering and started my career at Bombay Dyeing, where I worked in a textile manufacturing plant. During that time, I realized that managing processes and coordinating across teams to drive outcomes was something I really enjoyed.

This led me to pursue an MBA in Finance from Sydenham Institute (SIMSREE), Mumbai. Post-MBA, I joined HDFC Bank as a Product Manager in the auto loans division, where I got hands-on exposure to building and scaling digital journeys.

Currently, I own key parts of the Xpress Car Loan platform — particularly pre-approved and pre-qualified journeys. My role involves identifying drop-offs using data, designing end-to-end solutions, and collaborating with cross-functional teams like engineering, business, and operations to deliver measurable impact.

I worked on a journey where we identified a segment of customers eligible but not receiving offers due to data issues, and built an API-based solution to unlock that segment and improve conversions.
